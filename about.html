About RentHub

RentHub – “Rent Before You Buy” is a modern online rental marketplace designed to help people access premium electronics, photography gear, tools, and lifestyle equipment without the burden of full ownership.

Our mission is simple: promote sustainable consumption, save you money, and allow you to test-drive products in real life before committing to buying them.

Three Unified Platform Roles

Customer

Browse products, manage wishlist, request rental dates, and track active bookings.

Owner

List items with daily rates, review booking requests, accept/reject rentals, and manage inventory.

Admin

Full platform overview, user management, inventory moderation, booking oversight, and metrics.

Built with modern semantic HTML5, high-contrast CSS3 design system, and modular vanilla JavaScript utilizing browser localStorage for deterministic state persistence.
