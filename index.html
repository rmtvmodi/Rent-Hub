Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the RentHub rental experience.

How does renting on RentHub work?
Browse our catalog of verified equipment, pick your desired start and end dates, and submit a booking request. Once the item owner accepts your request, you can complete the simulated checkout to confirm and activate your rental.
How are rental payments handled?
RentHub features an integrated simulated checkout interface. When your booking is accepted, you can pay using simulated Card, UPI, or Net Banking options. Funds are held in escrow until pickup and return.
What happens if an item is returned past the due date?
If an item is returned after the agreed rental end date, the system automatically calculates a late return fee of 1.2× the daily rental rate for each overdue day. The owner inspects and confirms the return along with any assessed late fees.
Can I cancel my rental booking?
Yes! You can cancel any booking request free of charge directly from your Customer Dashboard while it is in "Requested" or "Accepted" status prior to payment.
How do I list my own products as an Owner?
Create an account with the "Owner" role or log into your existing owner profile. Access the Owner Dashboard to list gear, set daily rates, upload photos, manage availability, review booking requests, and track earnings.
